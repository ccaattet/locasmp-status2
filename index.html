NEED HELP?

Frequently asked questions.

How do I earn money?

Mine blocks. Your reward depends on the block's value, so more valuable blocks earn more money. Check your balance with /bal.

How do I get a rank?

Use /rank to explore the rank options configured on the server. Ranks are LuckPerms groups with different prefixes. You can switch between ranks you own using the server's rank system.

How do I teleport to my friends?

Use /tpa PLAYER to request to teleport to someone, or /tpahere PLAYER to request that they come to you. The other player can use /tpaccept to accept.

What is the Essence Shop?

It is a separate shop accessed with /essenceshop. Check the in-game menu for available items and their Essence costs.

How do I explore somewhere new?

Use /rtp for random teleportation, if available in your current situation. You can use /spawn to return to spawn.

How do I save my base location?

Use the server's home system with /home and any setup command or menu your server provides for saving homes.

Can I play on Bedrock Edition?

Bedrock support depends on the server's current Geyser and proxy configuration. Confirm the correct Bedrock connection address and UDP port before joining.
